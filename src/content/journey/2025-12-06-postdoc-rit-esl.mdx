## Staying, But Differently

Staying at the same institution for a postdoc means continuity of relationships and infrastructure — but the role is entirely different. As a postdoc, I have greater responsibility for the research agenda, mentorship, research direction, building collaborations, as well as grant search and management.

## Current Work

Current projects include continuing the re-development of the DeFake.app platform (a unified media forensics interface), continued refinement of the Dungeons & Deepfakes education platorm as well as new and exciting collaborations and projects around digital media forensics and provenance.
