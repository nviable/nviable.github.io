## Why LEVA

LEVA practitioners are responsible for authenticating video evidence in legal proceedings. The stakes are enormous — a falsely authenticated deepfake in a courtroom could determine someone's freedom. This workshop addressed not just technical detection, but the workflow integration, chain of custody, and decision-support needs of this community.
