Academic research rarely finds its way to general audiences without deliberate effort. The CNET interview was an opportunity to communicate the human stakes of deepfake detection — why usability matters, why journalists and analysts need dedicated tools, and what the DeFake project has built — to the millions of people who follow technology news.
