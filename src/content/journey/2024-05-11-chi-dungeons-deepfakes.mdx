## The Methodology

Traditional lab usability studies can't capture the pressure, bias, and context of real newsroom verification work. Dungeons & Deepfakes created an immersive tabletop role-play that placed journalists inside synthetic scenarios with real stakes — revealing behaviors that controlled experiments never surface.

## Impact

The study identified systematic biases in how journalists interpret AI-generated detection signals, and proposed design principles for verification tools that account for cognitive load, deadline pressure, and the social dynamics of newsroom decision-making. It has since been cited as a methodological contribution to HCI, not just a deepfake paper.
