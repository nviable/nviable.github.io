## The exhibit

**What Do You Mean by “Fake”? An Interactive Exploration of Synthetic Media** — with Kelly Wu, Ishmam Bin Rofi, Palavi Vinod Bhole, and Bryce Gernon.

<RemoteImage src={`${import.meta.env.PUBLIC_ASSETS_URL}/journey/2026-04-11/ims.jpg`} alt="What Do You Mean by “Fake”? An Interactive Exploration of Synthetic Media" />

Visitors defined terms via microphone or keyboard (live transcription into a shared definition pool), played **Is This Real?** on authentic vs. AI-generated images and videos with a leaderboard, then saw which “media persona” their definition resembled — highlighting how divergent everyday interpretations of “fake” and related labels really are.

## Why it matters

If labels like “deepfake” and “AI-generated” mean different things to different people, detection tools and policies have to account for that ambiguity — not assume a single shared mental model.
