## Significance

Being invited to brief DARPA represents a level of research impact that goes beyond academic publication. It means the work — a systematic ontology for how analysts reason about digital media evidence — was seen as relevant to real national security challenges.

## The Ontology

The Digital Media Forensics Ontology maps the concepts, tasks, evidence types, and reasoning patterns used by intelligence analysts when verifying media. It was co-developed with practitioners through interviews and workshops, making it grounded in real workflows rather than theoretical frameworks.
