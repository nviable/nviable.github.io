## The Human-Centered Turn

This research project was never purely technical deepfake detection and focused a lot on understanding journalists as users with parallel exploration of the technical aspects of deepfake detection. Through interviews and contextual inquiry, we mapped the real verification workflows journalists use — and identified the massive gap between what detection models output and what practitioners need to make decisions.

## What We Found

Journalists don't need a confidence score; they need an explanation. They don't trust black boxes. They work under deadline pressure. They're not ML researchers. These findings became the north star for every interface design decision in DeFake going forward.
