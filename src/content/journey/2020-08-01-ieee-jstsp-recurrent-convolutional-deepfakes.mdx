## Technical Depth

While the human-centered side of DeFake was developing, so was the underlying detection technology. This paper explored how combining recurrent and convolutional structures could improve detection accuracy across both audio spoof attacks and video-based deepfakes — a multimodal approach that was ahead of its time.
