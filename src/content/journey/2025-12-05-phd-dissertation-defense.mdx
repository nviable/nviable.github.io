## The Dissertation

The thesis brought together the full arc of the DeFake project: from understanding journalists' needs, to designing detection interfaces, to evaluating them through role-play and user studies, to applying cross-cultural lenses. It constitutes one of the first end-to-end human-centered accounts of AI-powered media verification tool design.

## What Completing It Felt Like

After seven years, the defense is simultaneously an ending and a beginning. The research questions don't stop — they multiply. But there's a clarity that comes from having completed the arc: knowing what the work is, what it contributed, and where it opens up.
