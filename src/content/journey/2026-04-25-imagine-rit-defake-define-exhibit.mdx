## DeFake @ ImagineRIT

On April 25, 2026, we ran our exhibit at Imagine RIT as part of the ESL Global Cybersecurity Institute programming in the exhibit hall.

<RemoteImage src={`${import.meta.env.PUBLIC_ASSETS_URL}/journey/2026-04-25/imagine-rit-2026-banner.png`} alt="DeFake Define exhibit banner at Imagine RIT 2026" />

The DeFake Define experience challenged visitors to identify deepfakes and also asked a harder question: what do people actually mean when they say "deepfake"? This is part of an ongoing user study to understand how the term is interpreted across different backgrounds as AI-generated media continues to evolve quickly.

We asked participants to define the term, clarify their stance, and then reflect on that definition after gameplay.

## Outcome

The exhibit won the 2026 Fram Award at Imagine RIT.

Our exhibit also appeared on live TV at 13 WHAM:

## Exhibit framing

AI-generated content is now a constant in our feeds and conversations, but the words we use for that media are not interpreted the same way by everyone. "What Do You Mean by 'Fake'?" invites people to compare those interpretations while competing to detect synthetic media.
