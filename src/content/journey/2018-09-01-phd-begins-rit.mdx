## A New Chapter

Moving from industry to academia as a Ph.D. student is a significant identity shift. At RIT, I found a lab environment that valued both technical rigor and human-centered thinking — exactly the intersection I wanted to explore. The central question that would define seven years of work: how do you build deepfake detection tools that practitioners can actually use?

## Why This Research

The deepfake problem was just beginning to reach public awareness. Journalists, law enforcement, and intelligence analysts would need tools to detect synthetic media — but who was thinking about making those tools usable? That gap became my dissertation.
