## The event

**Holding the Line: Authentication, Verification, and the Fight for Facts in the AI Age** — a Princeton CITP program co-sponsored with NYU Journalism and Princeton Journalism, held at NYU Journalism. The day mixed panels on threats and tools, concurrent workshops, and a brief technology showcase before a closing "big ideas" round and reception.

## On the ground

It was a good day for putting faces to names: people I've interviewed, collaborated with, or cited from afar, many for the first time in person. I gave a short presentation on existing verification technology from RIT's DeFake work; the demos held up in front of a live room, which was honestly a big relief given we pushed the beta live a couple of days prior and I updated the UI a day prior.

I left with the usual mix of optimism and impatience—more convinced that usability and trust for *end users* have to stay central if these tools are going to matter outside the lab, even when that means saying unpopular things in rooms full of experts. (What those opinions were stays in the Chatham-bound rooms ;).)
