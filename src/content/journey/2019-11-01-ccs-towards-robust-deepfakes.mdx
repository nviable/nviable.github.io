## The Work

This paper addressed a fundamental challenge: deepfake detectors trained on known datasets fail on new, unseen synthetic media. We explored approaches to make detection more robust in open-world conditions — a problem that remains active today.

## Why CCS Mattered

Publishing at CCS as a first-year Ph.D. student, even if it was a poster, provided early validation that this research direction was worth pursuing. It also connected the project to the security research community, not just the HCI world — a cross-disciplinary identity that has defined DeFake ever since.
