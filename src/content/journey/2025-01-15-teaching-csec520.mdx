## From Researcher to Educator

Teaching a graduate course is an exercise in what you actually understand versus what you think you understand. Teaching Cyberanalytics forced a different kind of clarity — not just knowing the material, but knowing how to sequence it, what to emphasize, and how to build intuition in students encountering it for the first time.

## The Approach

The course integrated practical ML workflows with real cybersecurity applications — the same intersection that defines the DeFake project. Students worked with real-world datasets and were encouraged to question model outputs rather than treat them as oracles.

## Misc

It really helped me review my own basics on machine learning and cybersecurity concepts, and go back to the roots having to explain these concepts to students. I remember dropping the ball at an interview the previous year because I forgot a basic concept.
