## Cross-Cultural Dimensions

This chapter extended Dungeons & Deepfakes to an international comparison — studying how journalists in the U.S. and Bangladesh differently approach AI-assisted verification. Cultural differences in institutional trust, risk tolerance, and technology access surfaced in ways that have direct implications for designing tools that work globally, not just in Western newsrooms.
