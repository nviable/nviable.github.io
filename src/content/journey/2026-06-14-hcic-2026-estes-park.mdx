## Theme

**AI’s Impact on Society** — design, evaluation, and critical perspectives on how intelligent systems reshape health, education, civic life, equity, and everyday experience. HCIC’s retreat format favors depth, debate, and sustained engagement with senior HCI researchers and partners.

## What’s different this time

The first HCIC was as a graduate student already deep into my primary research but still carving out a research identity. This year the goal was to contribute as a peer: same curiosity about digital forensics, but with way more opinions and perspectives after a good few years of running studies, shipping prototypes, and talking to journalists and analysts under real constraints.
