## Presentation

**Dungeons & Deepfakes: Interactive Simulation for Teaching Deepfake Detection Skills** — Group A2, 12-minute format, Bamboo CPC-2610, 10:15–11:15 AM. Presented as a postdoc in the Computing and Information Sciences program, Golisano College of Computing and Information Sciences.

## Focus

Deepfake verification training for newsrooms and classrooms: how interactive, role-play-style simulations surface workflow pressures, bias, and tool misuse — and what that implies for designing practice environments that match real verification work.
