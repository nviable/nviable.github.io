## Programme

The [AMAS Young Researcher Associate Programme](https://aiforgood.itu.int/multimedia-authenticity/amas-young-researcher-associate-programme/) connects early-career researchers with senior mentors on topics aligned with **AI and multimedia authenticity** — monthly cohort sessions and working groups aimed at shaping content integrity with emerging leaders in the field.

## Why it fits

The same thread as DeFake: how humans and institutions can reason about manipulated and synthetic media under real constraints — now with an explicit bridge to international standards and policy conversations through the ITU / AI for Good community.

## What excites me about it

It's an opportunity to connect and work with some great names in the field as well as a chance to get more involved in the international community of researchers and practitioners in the field of multimedia authenticity.
