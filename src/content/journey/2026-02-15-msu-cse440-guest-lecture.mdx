## Reaching the Next Generation

Teaching undergraduates about deepfakes is different from briefing intelligence analysts. The goal shifts from operational readiness to critical thinking — helping students understand how synthetic media works, why detection is hard, and what the sociotechnical stakes are before they enter a workforce that will have to navigate these tools.
