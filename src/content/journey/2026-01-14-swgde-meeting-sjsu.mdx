## Context

SWGDE brings together practitioners and experts who shape how digital evidence is understood and used in serious contexts. For synthetic-media and deepfake work, these conversations help align research tools with chain-of-custody, reporting, and reliability expectations in forensic settings.

## Goals

Listen for gaps between lab-ready detectors and what investigators and examiners need in real workflows — and feed that back into explainable, analyst-centered tool design.
