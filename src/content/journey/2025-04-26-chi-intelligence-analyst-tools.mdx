## The Analyst Context

Intelligence analysts have very different needs, mental models, and workflows than journalists. This study conducted deep contextual inquiry with analysts to understand how they reason about digital media evidence — and what a detection tool needs to do to fit into that reasoning process rather than disrupt it.

## Key Contributions

The paper proposed a set of analyst-centered design principles for deepfake detection interfaces, grounded in empirical findings. It also introduced an evaluation framework for measuring how well a tool supports analyst sense-making, not just detection accuracy.
