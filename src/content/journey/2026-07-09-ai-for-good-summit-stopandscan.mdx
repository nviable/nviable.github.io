## The session

[Session 1: AMAS Young Research Associate Programme](https://aiforgood.itu.int/event/ai-and-multimedia-authenticity-standards-2/) shares outcomes and lessons learned from the programme's first cohort — a year of mentored research on trust, authenticity, and rights in an era of AI-generated and AI-edited content, run under the ITU's AI and Multimedia Authenticity Standards (AMAS) collaboration.

## STOP&SCAN

With the social-sciences-and-education working group, I contributed to [STOP&SCAN](https://stopandscan.org) — a five-step sensemaking framework (Stop, then Source, Content, Align, Now-reflect), led by Fatma Aksu, that scaffolds how people reason through content that makes them suspicious instead of chasing a binary "is this real?" verdict. It leans on current multimedia-authenticity signals but organizes them into a sequence built for conscious decision-making and less bias — not another one-off detector.

I helped strengthen the framework as the senior on the group, then led the public-facing side: mascot design (meet **Amito**), branding and session messaging, the website itself, a cohort introduction video, and the learning interactions — while the tech and policy sub-teams drove their parts. Full write-up in [Projects](/projects/stop-and-scan).
