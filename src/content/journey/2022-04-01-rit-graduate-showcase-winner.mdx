## The Work Being Recognized

The Dungeons & Deepfakes study used an immersive role-play methodology to study how journalists behave when using AI verification tools — surfacing biases, workflows, and failure modes that lab experiments miss. The oral presentation format required distilling complex mixed-methods research into something compelling and accessible.

## What It Meant

Winning this award was a signal that the research was resonating beyond the research community — that the Dungeons & Deepfakes methodology had a story worth telling, and that I could tell it.
