## Reaching Practitioners

Law enforcement audiences require a different kind of talk than academic ones. The goal isn't to impress with methodology — it's to leave practitioners with actionable awareness, practical frameworks, and a clear-eyed view of what detection tools can and cannot do. This talk was designed for exactly that purpose.
