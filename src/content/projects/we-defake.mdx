## Case narrative

### Problem

Investigations span organizations and time zones, but mainstream social products are poor fits for forensic evidence handling, chain-of-custody norms, and expert-only discussion.

### Constraints

- Federation adds moderation, identity, and abuse-surface complexity
- Must interoperate with existing DeFake apps without forcing a single host
- Community size may stay small — design for depth over viral growth

### Approach

Deploy and customize Pleroma/Soapbox with workflows that encourage structured posts (claims, sources, tool outputs) and cross-link to DeFake analysis where appropriate. Iterate with pilot communities of researchers and journalists.

### Key decisions

**Use ActivityPub / Pleroma rather than a bespoke real-time-only stack** — Standards-based federation lets institutions run their own nodes and interoperate; reduces lock-in. Alternatives: closed Slack/Discord-style workspace only; greenfield custom social graph.

**Position We.DeFake as expert-adjacent, not a consumer social network** — Keeps expectations and moderation scope aligned with forensic use. Alternatives: open registration for general public.

### Tech stack

Pleroma, Soapbox, ActivityPub, Elixir, Docker.

- Federated communities need clear governance and onboarding for forensic norms
- Technical federation does not automatically create trust — social design matters
