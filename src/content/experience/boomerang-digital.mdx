## Research & national-scale UX

This chapter of my career was where product cadence and qualitative rigor had to coexist. Working with Nielsen Norman Group on the national health portal meant translating research reports into something a delivery team could ship — not as a one-off workshop, but as patterns we could reuse across flows.

## Product work

Health and livelihood products at national scale taught me how small interaction decisions compound — especially when users have uneven literacy, devices, and connectivity. I carried that lens forward into research: defaults, copy, and error states are never neutral.
