## DeFake Project

The through-line of my graduate years was building DeFake from an idea into a sustained collaboration: roadmap, funding conversations, recruiting across institutions, and keeping research questions honest to what practitioners said they needed — not only what was convenient to measure.

## Research practice

I ran studies that combined depth with pragmatism: longitudinal diary-style work when timelines allowed, targeted usability sessions when we needed fast iteration on prototypes, and field interviews when we needed to understand newsroom and analytic workflows on their own terms. The output was not only papers but also design arguments our engineering collaborators could act on.

## Design & communication

Figma prototypes were never decoration — they were probes we could put in front of people to stress-test assumptions about labels, timelines, and model explanations. Externally, I treated writing and speaking as part of the research pipeline: if a finding never leaves a lab slide deck, it cannot shift practice.
