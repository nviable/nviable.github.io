## Engineering & research

Chaldal was where I first owned both sides of the loop: shipping features and measuring whether they helped. Coupon flows were a concrete design surface; scheduling was where user mental models diverged most from what the backend assumed — a preview of the workflow problems I would later study in verification tools.

## Measurement culture

Building the A/B and analytics layer was as much about team habits as code: defining events people trusted, avoiding vanity metrics, and making experiment results legible to stakeholders who were not analysts by trade.
