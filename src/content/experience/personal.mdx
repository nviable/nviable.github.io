## A few things that shaped me

I grew up moving between very different places and languages — I speak English, Russian, and Bengali fluently, with intermediate Ukrainian and Hindi — which is probably why I think so much about how information travels, and how it gets bent along the way. I came up through art before tech, and I still lean on that side more than most researchers do.

Somewhere in there I also led what was, at the time, the best Counter-Strike: Source team in Bangladesh — as both the in-game leader and the analyst, writing strategies that a few US teams ended up borrowing. Which is more or less my whole personality in miniature: I'd rather invent my own approach than run the one everyone already knows.

## Off the clock

I'm married to the love of my life, who somehow stayed patient through the entire PhD — I'm endlessly grateful. We share a home with **Kyubi**, a cat with better manners than most people I've met.

I almost went pro in tennis as a kid, before life moved me to Bangladesh and I let it go. These days the competitive itch goes into games and esports instead: I follow Valorant (a Sentinels fan), F1 (a Ferrari one, against my better judgment), and Chelsea — and still lose plenty of sleep to matches of my own.
