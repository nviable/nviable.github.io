## Dissertation

The dissertation asked how journalist-facing verification tools should present model evidence so that people can reason with it — not be replaced by it. That question threaded through user studies, prototype iterations, and collaborations that eventually fed the broader DeFake roadmap.

## Committee & advising

I was fortunate to work with a committee that spanned HCI, journalism practice, security, and systems perspectives — a combination that kept the work honest about both human factors and deployment realities.

## Focus areas

Coursework and projects concentrated on HCI and usable security, media forensics and computer vision, and the ethics of automated decision support — the same triangle that still organizes my research today.
