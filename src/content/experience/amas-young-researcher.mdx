## Focus

The programme is a space to work on foundational challenges where “is it real?” is no longer a purely technical question — it is also social, organizational, and political. My contribution sits at the intersection of usability research and those larger stakes: how people are asked to trust signals, how interfaces encode assumptions, and what it would take for individuals to retain meaningful agency in verification.

## How this complements my other work

Alongside the DeFake initiative and postdoctoral work at RIT, AMAS YRAP helps keep long-horizon questions — about rights, authenticity, and the public interest — tied to concrete research artifacts rather than abstract debate alone.
