## Program

North South University was where I first connected formal methods, systems building, and questions about how people use software in the wild. The genetics minor was a deliberate counterweight — a reminder that “data” often comes from living systems and ethical contexts, not only from repositories.

## Projects

Early work on P2P conferencing and bibliomining taught me to care about end-to-end reliability and interpretability long before deepfakes were in the news. The bug-classification project was an early taste of ML in production settings where labels are noisy and incentives matter.
