## Projects

```markdown
## Projects

### DeFake Project — Deepfake Detection Platform *(Ongoing)*
**Lead Researcher & Founder** · [defake.app](https://defake.app/about) · [defake.app/about/projects](https://defake.app/about/projects)

The DeFake Project is an interdisciplinary research initiative at RIT's ESL Global Cybersecurity Institute, developing usable and interpretable tools for detecting AI-generated and manipulated media. The project is one of the eight winners of the **Knight Foundation's AI and the News Open Challenge** and has received over **$2 million in funding** from the NSF and Knight Foundation.

**Sub-projects:**
- **DeFake.app** — Flagship web application integrating ML-based deepfake detection (intra-frame, inter-frame, audio manipulation, speaker identity analysis) with a journalist-centered forensic interface
- **Varuna** — An all-in-one usable media forensics platform built around a goal-oriented Digital Media Forensics Ontology, developed with intelligence analysts and journalists
- **Dungeons & Deepfakes (D&DF)** — A scenario-based role-play methodology and web platform for studying and training journalists' deepfake detection workflows
- **We.DeFake** — A federated social media platform (ActivityPub/Pleroma) for collaborative media forensics among researchers, journalists, and fact-checkers

**Tools used:** Python, Flask, ReactJS, PyTorch, Keras, Figma, Docker, Pleroma/Soapbox
**Concepts:** Deep Learning, Usable Security, Media Forensics, UX Design, Collaborative Computing

---

### Dungeons & Deepfakes — Scenario-Based Role-Play Study *(2022–2024)*
**Principal Investigator**

An innovative role-play methodology—drawing on tabletop RPG conventions—for studying how journalists interact with AI-based verification tools under realistic deadline pressure. Conducted with **34 journalists** across the US and Bangladesh, uncovering automation bias, confirmation bias, and cross-cultural differences in tool trust. Awarded **1st place in Oral Presentation** at RIT Graduate Showcase 2022.

---

### Dungeons & Deepfakes Learning Platform *(2024–2025)*
**Lead Designer & Developer**

A simulation-based learning web application for journalism media verification training. Features **MaGe** (AI-assisted pedagogical scenario material generation), an interactive scenario interface, and post-scenario feedback with verdict drift graphs, action timelines, and verification competency quizzes.

---

### Digital Media Forensics Ontology / Varuna *(2023–Present)*
**Co-lead**

A DARPA-briefed, user-centered ontological framework for organizing digital media forensics tools, evidence types, and reasoning patterns — grounded in interviews with intelligence analysts and journalists.

---

### DartSessions — P2P Video Conferencing *(2013)*
A browser-based, registration-free P2P video conferencing platform with an intuitive interface.
**Tools:** WebRTC, NodeJS, PHP, HTML/CSS, JavaScript

---

### Tonic & CarrotCake — UX/UI Design *(2015)*
Carried out physical UX testing and developed wireframes and mobile/desktop UI for Telenor Health's *Tonic* health portal and *CarrotCake* doctors' patient-data interface, deployed nationally in Bangladesh.
**Tools:** Figma, Moqups, Canvasflip, Balsamiq, Illustrator
```
